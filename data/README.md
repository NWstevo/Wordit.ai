# Data

`kjv.json` is the King James Version (1769 text) in Wordit.ai's Bible format.

- Source: https://github.com/farskipper/kjv (`json/verses-1769.json`), public domain.
- Created with: `python -m wordit.kjv_import` after downloading the source to `data/raw/kjv-verses-1769.json`.
- Changes: paragraph marks (`#`) and italic brackets ([]) removed; "Solomon's Song" renamed to "Song of Solomon".
